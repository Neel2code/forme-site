Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026.

The short version

FORMÉ keeps your wardrobe on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics. We don’t sell data.

What stays on your device

Your photos, pieces, looks, plans, style answers, your name, your age range, and the name you give your Stylist are saved only on this iPhone. Deleting the app or using Reset FORMÉ in Profile removes them.

Photos and camera

You choose which photos to add using the system picker; FORMÉ only sees the ones you select. The camera is used only when you tap Take a photo. Removing a photo’s background and suggesting a category are done on your device.

Location and weather

If you turn on weather, FORMÉ asks for your approximate location once and uses it to fetch today’s weather from Open-Meteo. The coordinates are rounded to about 1 km and are not stored. They are also sent to Apple’s location service once to show the place name next to the weather. Nothing else is sent. You can turn weather off at any time.

Links

If you add a picture from a web link, your phone downloads that picture from the address you typed. Nothing about you or your closet is sent.

The Stylist

The Stylist runs on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device language model, on devices that support it. Your messages, your closet list and your style answers are used on the device to write replies and are not sent to FORMÉ or any server. Photos are never read by the Stylist. If a cloud AI service is ever added, FORMÉ will tell you what would be shared, ask first, and update this policy.

Age

The age range is optional and is used only to personalize suggestions. FORMÉ is not designed for children under 13.

Your choices

You can edit your style, turn personalization off, turn weather off, or delete everything from Profile.
